Config files

After editing config/machine.yaml (limits, motion) or config/cameras.yaml (camera rotation, resolution), apply the changes here. No restart is needed, so the cameras are not re-opened.

1 · Down camera scale

Home the machine, jog the down camera over something with texture (a PCB, a ruler or printed text) — not a plain or repeating pattern. The head then makes small moves and measures how far the image shifts, which gives the pixel-to-mm scale.

Scale: not calibrated

2 · Nozzle offsets

Camera on mark

Put a small mark (e.g. a dot on tape) on the bed and centre the down camera on it: click in the view or use Auto-centre on mark.

Mark: not recorded

Nozzle 1

Jog nozzle 1 down until it nearly touches the mark and centre it by eye, then raise it.

Offset N1: not recorded

Nozzle 2

Jog nozzle 2 down until it nearly touches the mark and centre it by eye, then raise it.

Offset N2: not recorded

2b · Prick needle offset

Centre the down camera on the mark (Camera is on mark, in step 2), lower the needle with Lower needle below, jog until the needle tip touches the mark, press Needle is on mark, then raise the needle. With the needle down, the head only moves when it was lowered here (or while a feeder is fed); lowering it from the Machine tab blocks all X/Y moves.

Prick offset: not recorded

3 · Up camera scale

Move nozzle 1 over the up camera so its tip is roughly centred (LEDs on). The head then makes small moves and measures how the tip shifts in the up view.

Scale: not calibrated

Place and Pick and place put the part at X/Y with this rotation (world degrees). With X/Y empty they use the camera crosshair. Depth empty = the default place depth.

Head

Click the view to move the head so that point is under the crosshair. To teach a feeder, jog or click until the crosshair is on the centre of the first pocket, then press Teach pick from camera. Home the machine first.

Holding: –

Prick: put the crosshair on the point where the needle enters the tape, then press Teach prick from camera. Drag feeders feed a pitch before each pick when needed. Tray: teach the first part with Teach pick from camera, then the last part (the opposite corner) with Teach last part from camera. Parts are taken row by row; no feed is needed.

Nozzles: –

Start places every pending part in order. Pause finishes the part in progress first. Skips and nozzle or feeder changes made while paused apply from the next part. Step (or the Step key on the machine) runs the job one stage per press: move over the lane, needle down and feed, needle up, move over the part, nozzle down, nozzle up, move to the place, place. Stop ends step mode and lifts the needle and nozzles.
