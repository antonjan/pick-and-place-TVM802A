    Config files

    After editing config/machine.yaml (limits, motion) or config/cameras.yaml (camera rotation, resolution), apply the changes here. No restart is needed, so the cameras are not re-opened.

    1 · Down camera scale

    Home the machine, jog the down camera over something with texture (a PCB, a ruler or printed text) — not a plain or repeating pattern. The head then makes small moves and measures how far the image shifts, which gives the pixel-to-mm scale.

    Scale: not calibrated

    2 · Nozzle offsets

    a) Camera on mark

    Put a small mark (e.g. a dot on tape) on the bed and centre the down camera on it (click in the view or use [Auto-centre on mark]).

    Mark: not recorded

    b) Nozzle 1

    Jog nozzle 1 down until it nearly touches the mark and centre it by eye, then raise it.

    Offset N1: not recorded

    c) Nozzle 2

    Jog nozzle 2 down until it nearly touches the mark and centre it by eye, then raise it.

    Offset N2: not recorded

    2b · Prick needle offset

    Centre the down camera on the mark ([Camera is on mark] in step 2), switch the prick needle on (Machine tab outputs or the button here), jog until the needle tip touches the mark, then [Needle is on mark], then switch the needle off.

    Prick offset: not recorded

    3 · Up camera scale

    Move nozzle 1 over the up camera so its tip is roughly centred (LEDs on). The head then makes small moves and measures how the tip shifts in the up view.

    Scale: not calibrated

    4 · Up camera positions

    Nozzle 1

    Centre nozzle 1 over the up camera (click the up view or use [Auto-centre tip]).

    Position N1: not recorded

    Nozzle 2

    Centre nozzle 2 over the up camera the same way, then record it.

    Position N2: not recorded
